Features

Everything around Roblox, in one place

Nexstrap starts the real Roblox client the supported way. Everything below works around it, never inside it.

Installs and updates Roblox

Downloads the official Roblox Player from Roblox's own servers, checks every file, and updates it in the background while Roblox is closed.

Find games and servers

Search games by name, browse public servers by players and ping, and rejoin the server you just left.

Play stats

Play time per game, your top games, the last 14 days and streaks. Stored only on your PC.

Make it yours

Dark or light, any accent colour and font, your own background, glass cards, a custom sidebar and a loading screen you design.

Discord Rich Presence

Shows the game you're in on your Discord profile, with its icon and a View game button. Every part can be switched off.

Performance settings

Frame-rate cap, graphics quality and presets in Roblox's own settings, plus FastFlags labelled honestly by Roblox's allowlist.

Roblox Studio

Open Studio and install or update it straight from Roblox, with the same file checks as the Player.

Crash alerts and reminders

If Roblox closes suddenly, rejoin the same server in one click. An optional reminder tells you how long you've played.

Six languages

English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Português and Русский, following Windows by default.

Welcome

A home screen that follows your clock

Nexstrap greets you by the time of day with a landscape for morning, afternoon, evening and night. It only reads your PC's clock, so no location is used.

Appearance

Change every corner

  • Themes, presets and any accent colour
  • Your font, background image, blur and card style
  • Rename the sidebar, add your own logo, hide or reorder pages
  • Save themes and share them as a file

Servers

Pick the server, not just the game

  • Browse public servers with player counts and ping
  • Hide full servers or ask for free slots for your friends
  • Try to land in a region, like Frankfurt (experimental)

Loading screen

Your own start-up screen

  • Your text, colours, logo and background
  • Spinner, bar, pulse or bouncing dots
  • Size, position, corners and a fade

Safety

Built to stay out of the way

Nexstrap launches Roblox the same way Windows does. Sign-in, servers and anti-cheat are all handled by Roblox.

Never touches your account

No passwords, no cookies, no sign-in. Nexstrap only uses Roblox's public information.

Official Roblox files only

Roblox is downloaded unmodified from Roblox's own servers, and every package is checked against Roblox's checksums.

Your data stays on your PC

Settings, stats and themes live in %LocalAppData%\Nexstrap. Online, Nexstrap only asks Roblox's public services and GitHub (for updates), and uploads nothing unless you choose a Discord card image.

Verified updates

Nexstrap only updates itself from its own GitHub releases, and checks the file's SHA-256 before installing.

FAQ

Questions

Is Nexstrap free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT licence, and the code is on GitHub.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes. Nexstrap isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it at first. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the download against the SHA-256 in each release's SHA256SUMS.txt.

Can I get banned for using it?

Nexstrap starts the official client the normal way and doesn't inject anything or touch anti-cheat, and Roblox ignores any FastFlag that isn't on its own allowlist. Only Roblox decides on bans, so no launcher can promise anything, but Nexstrap stays inside what Roblox allows.

Will it make Roblox faster?

It can't make your PC faster. It can change Roblox's frame-rate cap and graphics quality, which helps if they were set too low or too high for your PC.

Does it work with Bloxstrap, Fishstrap or Voidstrap installed?

Yes. Nexstrap can use Roblox builds those launchers installed, but never changes or deletes them. Turning off "Open website Play buttons with Nexstrap" gives Roblox links back to the launcher you used before.

How do I uninstall it?

From Installed apps in Windows Settings. It asks whether to keep your settings.

Something doesn't work. Where do I ask?

Open an issue on GitHub. Copy diagnostics on the About page adds the details that help.
